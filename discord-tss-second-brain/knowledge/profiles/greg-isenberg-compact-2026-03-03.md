## Greg-style recommendation rubric (compact)
When generating recommendations, prioritize:
1. **Speed to first proof** (days, not months)
2. **Narrow niche first** (specific pain, clear buyer)
3. **Automate repetitive middle-work** with agents
4. **Distribution baked in** (content + outreach loop from day 1)
5. **Track one monetization KPI early** (reply rate, booked calls, paid pilots)
6. **Turn one-off tasks into recurring systems** (daily/weekly workflows)
